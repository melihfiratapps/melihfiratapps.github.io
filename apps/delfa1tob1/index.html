Overview

French vocabulary for DELF A1, A2 and B1, organised by level so you can work through one at a time instead of drowning in a single list. Each word is tracked as not started, learning or learned, and practice mode makes you produce the answer rather than recognise it. Cards and progress stay on your device, so it works on the métro.

Questions

Before you download DELF A1 to B1: Flashcards.

Is this an official DELF app?
No. DELF A1 to B1: Flashcards is an independent study app built by Melih Firat. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to France Éducation international.
Which levels does it cover?
A1, A2 and B1 — the three levels the DELF diploma covers at the lower end of the CEFR scale, kept as separate word sets.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The cards and your progress are stored on the device, so nothing needs a connection.
What does Pro unlock?
The complete A1, A2 and B1 word sets, plus the premium features. The app is free to download and part of each level is free.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad, on iOS. There is no Android or web version.
