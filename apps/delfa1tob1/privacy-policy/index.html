Privacy Policy for DELF A1 to B1: Flashcards

Effective date: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the DELF A1 to B1: Flashcards app (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Important Disclaimer

This App is an independent, unofficial application developed by Melih Firat. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by France Éducation international or any associated entities. This App has no connection to France Éducation international or the DELF/DALF exams whatsoever.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Subscriptions: When you purchase a Pro subscription, we collect payment information through Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • Support requests: Any contact via email or support forms.

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (OS, version, device type)
  • Subscription status and entitlements (via RevenueCat)
  • Usage (session, in-app actions)
  • Progress data on your device:
    • Words: learned, learning, not started
    • Flashcard and study progress, including practice or test activity in the App
  • App preferences and settings

3.3 Information Stored Locally

  • Learning progress (e.g., vocabulary)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Content cached for offline use

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 RevenueCat

  • Handles subscription purchases and verification
  • Privacy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4.2 Apple App Store / Google Play Store

  • Process payments
  • Manage subscriptions
  • Subject to Apple/Google privacy policies

4.3 AsyncStorage (Local Storage)

  • Stores progress on your device only
  • No transmission to external servers

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Enable Pro features
  • Verify subscription status
  • Improve content and features
  • Troubleshoot
  • Process transactions
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your data. Analytics is limited to improving the service.

6. Data Storage and Security

Most progress is stored locally and controlled by you.

Subscription and payment data are handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.

We limit data transmission and rely on established platform payment systems.

Protection measures:

  • Local data encryption where applicable
  • Secure transmission via standard protocols
  • Regular updates

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Limited sharing includes:

  • RevenueCat, Apple, and Google for payment/verification
  • Legal requirements or protection of rights

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • View and update progress at any time
  • Cancel subscriptions in your device settings
  • Restore prior purchases within the App
  • Delete local data by uninstalling (or use in-app reset if available)
  • Request data access by contacting support

9. Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Parents should supervise usage and review our policies.

10. International Users

Data may be processed outside your country.

Usage implies consent to processing as described in this Privacy Policy.

11. Data Retention

  • Local progress: retained while the App is installed
  • Subscription data: retained while active and for a reasonable period after expiry
  • Support communications: retained to assist and for record-keeping

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Updates take effect when posted. Continued use after changes implies acceptance.

13. Your Consent

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.
