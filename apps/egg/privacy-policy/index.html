Egg Timer: Perfect egg boiler - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 6 November 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Egg Timer ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

  • Developer: Melih Firat
  • App Name: Egg Timer
  • Version: 1.0.0

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Egg Timer does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

The App collects minimal information locally on your device:

  • Timer Preferences: Your selected egg doneness (soft, medium, hard) or custom timer duration
  • Timer State: Current timer status and remaining time (if a timer is running)
  • App Settings: Your app preferences and settings

All this information is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers or external systems.

2.3 Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you alerts when your egg timer completes
  • Audio: To play sound alerts when the timer finishes
  • Storage: To save your preferences locally on your device

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Remember your timer preferences between app sessions
  • Restore timer state if the app is closed or interrupted
  • Provide personalized timer functionality
  • Improve your user experience within the App

We do not use your information for:

  • Marketing communications
  • Sharing with third parties (except as described below)
  • Analytics or tracking beyond basic App functionality

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Egg Timer uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

What AdMob May Collect:

Google AdMob may collect and use information about your device and usage patterns, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • Device type and operating system
  • General location information (country/city level)
  • App usage data
  • IP address

How AdMob Uses This Information:

This information is used by Google to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal requirements

Your Options:

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

  • On iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > turn off Personalized Ads
  • On Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

For more information about Google's data practices, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy

4.2 App Store Services

When you download the App from Apple App Store or Google Play Store, those platforms may collect information according to their respective privacy policies. We do not control this data collection.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

All user preferences and timer data are stored locally on your device using secure local storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

5.2 Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data by:

  • Uninstalling the App from your device
  • Clearing the App's data through your device settings

5.3 Security

While we implement reasonable security measures for local data storage, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • AdMob: As described in Section 4.1, Google AdMob may collect information for advertising purposes
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of users or others

7. Children's Privacy

Egg Timer is designed for users of all ages. However, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information from our systems.

If you are under 13 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction), please obtain parental consent before using the App.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Access: You can view all data stored locally on your device through the App
  • Deletion: You can delete all local data by uninstalling the App or clearing App data
  • Permission Control: You can grant or revoke permissions (notifications, audio) through your device settings
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (see Section 4.1)

9. International Users

The App is available globally. If you are using the App from outside [Your Country], please note that:

  • Your data is stored locally on your device
  • AdMob may process data in accordance with Google's international data transfer practices
  • Your use of the App constitutes consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy
