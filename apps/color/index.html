Color Picker

Capture colours with your camera

Download on the App Store

Overview

Point your camera at anything and capture precise colours in real time. Get HEX, RGB and HSL values instantly, save palettes, and export them for your design tools.

What’s inside

Everything in Color Picker.

  • Live camera colour picker with crosshair sampling

  • Exact HEX, RGB and HSL values

  • Save favourite colours and build palettes

  • Name detection for common colours

  • Import and analyse screenshots and photos

  • Copy to clipboard with one tap
