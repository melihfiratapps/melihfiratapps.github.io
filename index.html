How they are built

Practice that matches the paper.

The real format

Every practice set follows the published exam structure — the same parts, the same task types, the same time limits. Nothing invented.

All four skills

Reading, listening, writing and speaking in one app. Audio for every listening task, model answers for every written one.

Works on the train

Everything downloads to the device. No account, no sign-up, and your progress never leaves your phone.

Independent, not official

Study apps, not certificates.

These are unofficial trainers built by one developer. They rehearse the published format of each exam so nothing on test day is a surprise, but they are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to telc gGmbH, the Goethe-Institut, France Éducation international, Instituto Cervantes, the British Council, IDP, or Cambridge Assessment English. Book your exam with the awarding body itself.
